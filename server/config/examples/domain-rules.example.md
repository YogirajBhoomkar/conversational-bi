The data is reinsurance placement and capacity analytics.
- "Year" means Underwriting Year.
- Placement statuses go in workflow order: Request Quote, Provide Quote, FOT, Authorization, Signed (not alphabetical). Ask Genie for that order.
- Years ascending for trends; rankings by value descending.
- Money in tables and answers is shown in USD millions with one decimal.
- Charts are for reinsurance analysts: each chart answers one question they would act on. Money measures (limit, premium, authorized, signed, unused capacity) can share a chart; counts (declines to quote, placements) go on their own chart, never on a money axis. A gap such as authorized against signed limit reads best as bars side by side per region or line of business; the flow of placements through the status workflow reads best as a sankey or funnel.
